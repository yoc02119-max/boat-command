# Venue-specific point expansion experiment

Current PROGRAM_ONLY picks, probabilities, model weights, UI, TRY and money handling remain unchanged. At FIRST creation of a future PROGRAM_ONLY snapshot, preserve the complete 120-order distribution and six frozen variants. Existing snapshots are never enriched retrospectively. A missing or malformed distribution skips this auxiliary experiment without blocking baseline generation.

BASE4 is the original four. RANK6/RANK8 append ranks 5–6/5–8. HEAD6 appends the two highest-ranked unused tickets whose winner is not represented in BASE4. SECOND6 prioritizes represented winners with an unrepresented first/second pair. THIRD6 prioritizes represented first/second pairs with another third. If fewer than two preferred tickets exist, fill with the highest-ranked unused tickets. All policies are fixed before outcomes. They are research alternatives, not user purchase instructions.

All supported generators record the same alternatives; comparisons are separated by venue, model version and configuration hash. No automatic policy choice or promotion. The September 22 observations are exploratory hypotheses only: head coverage in Kiryu/Tokoname/Kojima/Wakamatsu, second coverage in Edogawa/Tokuyama, third coverage in Miyajima, mixed errors elsewhere. These labels do not change predictions or select a winner. Venues without new eligible snapshots remain WAITING, never counted as zero-hit venues. The 24-venue report does not claim every venue uses this generator; legacy or incompatible models require a separate adapter.

The reporter only consumes frozen variants and matching result records. It checks venue/date/race, generation at least three minutes before deadline, valid 120-order distribution, exact baseline prefix, deterministic variant consistency and research boundaries. It never calls a model after reading results. It excludes old snapshots without the expansion field. Today's unknown ranks 5–8 must not be reconstructed and presented as forward evidence.
